# Self Reflection

## What went well?
- I think the idea of the game and making the first "prototype" went pretty well
- Getting the AI to know what I was talking about was a bit easier, considering "The Backrooms" are pretty well known lately.
- Getting the AI to understand my prompts and my way of talking, it seemed to sort of "adapt" to it which was very interesting to see.

## What went less good?
- I realized that sometimes the AI backtracked on itself, fixing a script, and with the next fix he used the older one again, which meant a constant loop of fixing the same issues.
- Asking the AI for simpler/smaller tasks would've been better I assume, since everytime I made it do a bigger task, it would mess something up.
- I very often focused on already adding more, before making sure the rest was perfect/stable, which was a mistake on my end.

## What did I learn by this myself?
- I learned that AI has learned a lot in the coding scale, which in my eyes is kind of scary considering it also has my future on the line in a way.
- AI can make almost exactly what you want, aslong as you explain it well and detailed enough.
- Telling AI to do something is easy, but getting a good result needs good explanation. If you don't, it's basically asking someone who's only heard a description but has never seen a dog, to suddenly draw it.
